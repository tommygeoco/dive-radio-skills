Designer, Web Presence & Platform — Stripe (source https://stripe.com/careers/listing/designer-web-presence-platform/8130913, read Sep 17, 2026)
About the team: Web Presence & Platform (WPP) has two pillars, Platform (internal systems, tooling, infrastructure for the web) and Presence (Stripe's public-facing storytelling across stripe.com: homepage, product landing pages, industry and solutions pages, go-to-market surfaces). Designers on the Presence team balance clarity, polish and systems thinking with strategic storytelling and experimentation; collaborate with engineering, marketing, content and product; deliver work that measurably moves the business.
What you'll do: contribute to strategy and execution of Stripe's most visible web surfaces; design new product pages; evolve storytelling for specific industries or audiences; refine reusable patterns that scale across stripe.com. "Takes pride in the pixels." Work shapes how millions of users understand Stripe and converts interest into action, from founders to enterprise CTOs.
Responsibilities: design new and improved experiences across stripe.com (product, industry, solution landing pages); collaborate with engineers, PMMs and content designers to define problems and explore solutions; partner with other designers to maintain and evolve systems that uphold Stripe's design standards at scale; balance storytelling with usability; use user research, user testing and data to guide and validate decisions; participate in critique; meticulous attention to craft.
Minimum requirements: 2–4 years of relevant design experience, ideally web or product marketing; a strong portfolio of web design work demonstrating exceptional visual design skills, systems thinking and attention to detail; ability to independently scope and lead small to medium projects; strong communication, collaborative mindset; fluency in layout, typography, color, hierarchy and interaction fundamentals; comfortable in Figma and component-based systems; experience with CMS-driven websites and modular systems.
Preferred: familiarity with front-end development workflows or ability to speak fluently with engineers; web performance, accessibility and SEO; international/multilingual sites; fast-paced cross-functional environments.
How we work: craft plus urgency and focus; feedback early and often; users front and center; curiosity, humility, resilience. Growth path: Presence designers have gone on to lead launches and take leadership roles.
Remote: a remote location is 35+ miles from a Stripe office; expectation is regular work from home; Stripe does not cover relocation to a remote location; apply for roles matching where you live.
Pay: annual US base salary range $133,200–$199,800; may span several career levels, narrowed in interview by experience, qualifications and location. Additional benefits may include equity, company bonus, 401(k), medical/dental/vision, wellness stipends.
Application window open 100 days after posting; may close earlier or later.
