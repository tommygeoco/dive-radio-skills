Founding Designer — Blacksky Algorithms (posted Sep 11, 2026; source https://blackskyweb.xyz/about/jobs/founding-designer/)
PRODUCT, BRAND, AND VISUAL IDENTITY. Blacksky Algorithms is building an interconnected suite of products: BLACKSKY, an open-source social network on the AT Protocol (45 million users), ACORN community management software, and Blacksky Cash, a peer-to-peer payments app.
About the role: We're looking for a Founding Designer to lead and shape how our products and brands look, feel, and work. Highly hands-on, multidisciplinary. You'll work closely with product, engineering, community, and our founder, with significant ownership over both the experiences we build and the visual direction of the company.
What you'll do: Product (80%): lead end-to-end design for Blacksky's core mobile-first social experience; own product design direction across Acorn and Blacksky Cash; turn ambiguous problems into clear flows through research, prototyping, iteration; build and maintain the design system engineering builds against, hold the bar on what ships; partner with engineering early. Brand and marketing (20%): evolve Blacksky's visual identity and give Acorn and Blacksky Cash identities that read as one family; produce marketing material; write brand guidelines a non-designer can follow. Building the function: establish how design works here (critique, handoff, review cadence); tell us when we need more design capacity, and help hire it.
What we're looking for: 4+ years designing consumer digital products, mobile-first, portfolio of shipped work showing end-to-end ownership; strong graphic and visual craft alongside interaction design; experience developing a visual identity, ideally for more than one product; product judgment; comfortable being the only designer, you scope your own work and don't wait for a brief; clear communicator; based in New York City and able to work from our Brooklyn office at least 3 days a week.
Nice to have: social/community/messaging/feeds/payments experience; design systems; motion or video editing for social; enough HTML and CSS to prototype in code or read a pull request; familiarity with open source / ATmosphere.
Who this isn't for: folks looking to manage a team on day one; designers who need a mature product org, a dedicated researcher, or a PM handing over specs.
Compensation and logistics: $100,000–$150,000 base depending on experience, plus equity. Full-time. Remote, with significant overlap with eastern time. Reports to the Founder & CEO.
How to apply: link to your portfolio and a short note on one thing you've designed that you're proud of and what you'd change about it now. A portfolio is required; a degree is not.
